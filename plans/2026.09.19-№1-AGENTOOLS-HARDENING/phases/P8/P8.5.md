**Implementation Rules:** Portable gates own exact input/output byte limits, depth/count/page/query/row caps, peak-resource ceilings supported by the runtime and growth-class envelopes; they do not depend on a faster host. Timing receipt records commit, OS/kernel/arch/CPU/core/memory/container, power/governor when observable, PHP/Dart/PostgreSQL/tool/dependency versions, command/config/input digests, warmup, raw samples and summary statistics. Timing is blocking only against a reviewed baseline with matching fingerprint and workload digest; otherwise it is labeled `informational`/`baseline_pending`. Dart `benchmark_harness` is exact-lock dev tooling, and its one-call exercise semantics are explicit. PHP and Dart timing are never compared. Critical correctness rows cannot be quarantined; any noncritical quarantine names owner, lost coverage, reason and expiry. One rerun may classify infrastructure noise only with unchanged seed/fingerprint and both raw attempts retained.
**Code Guidance:** Run each workload in a fresh bounded subprocess where possible. Stream corpora and measure before/at/over-bound inputs plus geometric size series so superlinear regressions are visible without trusting one timestamp. Keep contention timing separate from storage correctness. Aggregate by exact `AV-###` sets and child receipt digests; never sum semantic, mutation, fuzz, storage and performance results into one percentage. Emit one producer-readiness candidate for P9/P10 only when every applicable critical row is green.
