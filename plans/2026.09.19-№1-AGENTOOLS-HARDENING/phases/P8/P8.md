# P8 — Adversarial verification и resource envelopes

## Phase Context

P8 превращает гарантии P2–P7 в failure-oriented доказательства: один versioned
risk/proof manifest связывает каждую security/durability boundary с независимым oracle,
детерминированным corpus, обязательным fault model и воспроизводимым receipt. Фаза ищет
ложно-зелёные проверки, а не добавляет ещё один happy-path regression layer. `RAG:—`.

PHP получает scoped Infection campaign и набор обязательных semantic mutants. Dart получает
тот же language-neutral fault catalog через digest-bound patches в disposable tree; regex-based
generic mutation score не выдаётся за эквивалент AST mutation. Property/fuzz cases и storage
schedules имеют стабильные seeds, generator/schedule versions и promoted minimized regressions.
PostgreSQL проверяется реальными процессами и соединениями, без SQLite substitution и sleep-based
ordering. Resource gates отделяют portable deterministic limits от осторожно сравнимого только на
том же fingerprint timing evidence. `RAG:✅` в `research/P8-design-rag.md`.

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: terminal guarantees/contracts/receipts P1–P7.
> Производит: adversarial-verification contract, risk/proof manifest, deterministic corpus,
> mutation and PostgreSQL schedule evidence, calibrated resource baselines и aggregate P8 receipt.
> Границы: тест не переписывает upstream semantics; P9 владеет CI/workflow wiring, P10 — RC acceptance.

## Scope Included

Versioned risk-to-proof matrix; deterministic language-neutral generators and shrinkers; raw-invalid,
metamorphic and cross-language differential corpora; PHP Infection campaign; curated critical
semantic mutants for both SDKs; real PostgreSQL multi-process schedules with observable barriers,
client/backend crash modes and clock-skew cases; parser/page/contention/resource envelopes;
environment fingerprints, raw benchmark samples, flake/quarantine rules and aggregate receipts.

## Scope Excluded

New product semantics or guarantee wording; automatic weakening of an upstream contract after RED;
SQLite/in-memory evidence as proof of PostgreSQL arbitration; indefinite fuzzing service; formal
verification; one global coverage/MSI/latency number; comparing PHP and Dart speed; CI workflows,
runner fleet or schedule ownership; public release/tag/upload; server/power-loss WAL certification.
