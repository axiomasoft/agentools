# P5 — Atomic event store, audit envelope и observability

## Phase Context

P5 заменяет caller-assembled `EventLog.append(ActionEvent)` на storage-assigned atomic batch
`EventStore`, content-aware event-id collision, paginated load, redacted audit export и
low-cardinality operational signals. Журнал остаётся единственным источником истины действия;
snapshot — проекция с provenance.

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: P1 threat/guarantees/resource policy.
> Производит: EventStore API, uncommitted/committed envelopes, collision/redaction/cardinality
> rules, durable PostgreSQL adapter, pagination/snapshot provenance, audit export policy,
> observability contract и documented guarantees. Границы: metrics не получают unbounded labels;
> journal не заменяется snapshot; P3 consume и P4 lease не сливаются в event rows; shared
> spec/error revision — P6; final exports — P7; mutation/fuzz/page-size depth — P8.

> **Phase Assurance | v1.**
> Impact: public-contract
> Integration Seams: action-journal · event-store · postgresql-pdo · snapshots · audit-sink · telemetry
> Live or Irreversible: no
> Audit Intent: direct-close-eligible
> Rationale: expected-version, duplicate collision, atomic batch, pagination и redaction
> доказываются concurrent/integration tests; Review=full.

## Scope Included

Uncommitted/committed event types; batch append; stream expected version; storage-assigned sequence and
global position; identical duplicate success vs different-payload collision vs partial overlap;
schema version; tenant/actor/trace/correlation/causation metadata placement; paginated load;
snapshot provenance; safe audit envelope; redaction; metrics/tracing/logging semantics; PHP/Dart
parity и durable adapter tests; wiring `JournalAppender` / `AuditEventFactory` through EventStore.

## Scope Excluded

Mandatory event sourcing/CQRS platform; arbitrary projections engine; high-cardinality tenant/action
labels; raw tokens/arguments/prompts/PII in audit; replacement of the business database; exactly-once
consumer delivery; editing `spec/revisions/2026-08/**`; P3 token consume; P4 lease/fencing; final
package exports; public release; live owner-database migration; OpenTelemetry SDK runtime dependency.
