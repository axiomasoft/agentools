### P3.1 — Заморозить token-replay contract, migration и adversarial cases

**Intent:** Выпустить versioned P3 contract, который задаёт `at1` envelope, parser bounds, key lifecycle, presentation-consume ordering, retention/cleanup и общий набор PHP/Dart adversarial cases.
**Why:** Current tokens omit `v`/`alg`/`kid`/`iss`/`aud`/`jti`/`tenant`/`purpose`, spend into process memory, and parse JSON without duplicate-name or size gates. F02/F06 are confirmed; audit SQL is not the schema (F14). `RAG:—`; внешние ограничения — `research/P3-design-rag.md`.
**Scope Included:** Envelope and signing-input profile; closed claim set; algorithm/key-state/purpose enums; consume timing versus pre-presentation failures; outcome vocabulary; jti versus raw-token identity; clock skew and numeric bounds; PHP/Dart migration of old two-segment tokens; machine-readable positive/negative case IDs.
**Scope Excluded:** Runtime implementation; PostgreSQL driver choice beyond injected SQL; KMS; editing `spec/revisions/2026-08/**`; lease/event-store APIs; final exports; public release.
