### P10.4 — Собрать final evidence dossier и адъюдицировать residual risk

**Intent:** Independently exact-join P1–P10 evidence into a final dossier and assign every residual one explicit owner, impact, disposition and release-axis consequence.
**Why:** Aggregate PASS without residual ownership can hide baseline-pending timings, unavailable hosted capabilities, non-applicable MCP rows or accepted non-guarantees behind one green label. `RAG:—`.
**Scope Included:** Recompute P10.1 matrix over current bytes; final threat/guarantee/support/API/conformance/adversarial/supply-chain/doc/consumer coverage; residual-risk ledger; private/public blocker sets; accepted non-guarantees; capability and point-in-time evidence ages; exact subject/source/tree identity; deterministic dossier schema/renderer; independent corruption and omission checks.
**Scope Excluded:** Fixing product defects; changing contracts or acceptance thresholds; owner acceptance of legal/public risk; publishing; adding a second semantic audit; collapsing child evidence into a single score.
**Inputs:** P10.1 contract/matrix · P10.2 docs receipt · P10.3 consumer receipt · all P1–P9 terminal receipts and named aggregate artifacts · current decisions/open questions · exact source/tree and P9 subject digests.
**Files:** `tools/release_dossier.py` · `tests/tools/test_release_dossier.py` · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/artifacts/P10-release-dossier.json` · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/findings/P10-release-dossier.md` · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/findings/P10-residual-risks.md`.
**Required Reads:** 1) P10.1–P10.3 outputs 2) P1 threat model and guarantee/non-guarantee/support/resource contracts 3) P2–P5 security/durability terminal evidence 4) P6 separate semantic/JCS/MCP reports 5) P7 API/package report 6) P8 adversarial/resource aggregate 7) P9 CI/reproducibility/SBOM/provenance/private-RC aggregate 8) decisions D4, D5, D11–D15 and current open questions.
**Implementation Rules:** Recompute rather than trust rendered summaries. Every residual row includes stable ID, originating evidence, affected subject/API, severity/impact, owner, disposition, private/public blocker booleans, expiry/review trigger and supporting digest. `accepted-non-guarantee` cannot hide a failed required guarantee. `baseline_pending`, `capability_unavailable`, scanner age and non-applicability stay visible. Private readiness is GREEN only with zero unresolved private blockers and all required evidence fresh. Public readiness is eligibility only, never authorization, and stays false while D5 or the MCP hosted topology is unresolved.
**Code Guidance:** Treat all aggregate child receipts as untrusted inputs and validate schemas, digests, exact-set membership and source/tree coherence. Render tables from JSON. Mutate each relation in fixtures, including swapped subject hashes, stale source tree, omitted residual, promoted timing result, merged MCP percentage, false attestation and an owner-decision row silently marked accepted.
**Validation:** Dossier schema/fixtures are green; exact-set coverage of P10.1 claims and residuals has missing/extra=[]; all source/tree/subject/receipt digests cohere; every private blocker is resolved and every remaining public blocker is explicit; corruption/omission/promotion fixtures fail; human dossier is generated from JSON and contains no hidden aggregate score; no product/decision/external state changes; plan lint adds no ERROR.
**Deliverables:** Machine-readable final release dossier, rendered evidence report and complete residual-risk ledger with separate private-readiness and public-eligibility verdicts.
