### P10.1 — Заморозить release-acceptance contract и exact claim/evidence matrix

**Intent:** Создать единственный нормативный release-acceptance contract и exact machine-readable matrix, связывающую каждый P1 guarantee/non-guarantee, support row, semantic/API claim и release subject с его текущим evidence owner.
**Why:** Набор зелёных phase receipts не доказывает, что публичные обещания полны, непротиворечивы и относятся к одним bytes; P10 нельзя сводить к общему CI status. `RAG:—` в `research/P10-design.md`.
**Scope Included:** Private/public acceptance axes; closed status taxonomy; exact claim and evidence IDs; upstream owner/path/digest relations; P1 threat/guarantee/support/resource rows; P2–P5 security/durability receipts; P6 semantic/JCS/MCP namespaces; P7 API/package rows; P8 adversarial/resource rows; P9 subject/SBOM/provenance/capability rows; release-blocker/residual-risk rules; deterministic validator and adversarial fixtures.
**Scope Excluded:** Re-running child suites; changing upstream semantics or receipts; user-facing documentation; consumer execution; risk acceptance; public version/channel/legal choice; tags, uploads or credentials.
**Inputs:** P1–P9 terminal item/phase receipts and declared artifacts · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/root/contracts/hardening-guarantees-v1.md` · `root/contracts/compatibility-support-v1.md` · `root/contracts/resource-envelope-v1.md` · `spec/revisions/2026-09/semantic-manifest.json` · `api/package-surface-v1.json` · `artifacts/P8-adversarial-gate.json` · `artifacts/P9-private-rc.json` · D4, D5, D11–D15.
**Files:** `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/root/contracts/release-acceptance-v1.md` · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/root/contracts/release-acceptance-v1.schema.json` · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/artifacts/P10-acceptance-matrix.json` · `tools/release_acceptance.py` · `tests/tools/test_release_acceptance.py` · `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/findings/P10-acceptance-matrix.md`.
**Required Reads:** 1) `plans/2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING/research/P10-design.md` and `research/P10-design-rag.md` 2) P1 threat, compatibility/support, guarantee and resource contracts 3) P2–P5 terminal receipts and residual handoffs 4) P6.5 integrated receipt including separate Agentools/JCS/MCP sections 5) P7.4, P8.5 and P9.5 aggregate receipts with exact child digests 6) D4, D5, D11–D15 and current open questions.
**Implementation Rules:** Derive the claim set from normative upstream owners; never hand-copy a smaller allowlist. Each row has stable ID, class, owner, evidence path/digest, applicability, freshness, private-blocking and public-blocking dispositions. Closed states distinguish `verified`, `non_applicable`, `baseline_pending`, `capability_unavailable`, `decision_pending`, `failed` and `stale`; only contract-declared states may be non-blocking on each axis. Keep Agentools, JCS and official MCP scenario compatibility separate. Exactly-once external effect, certification and unavailable hosted attestation are forbidden claims. A private PASS never sets `public_authorized=true`.
**Code Guidance:** Validate JSON structurally and semantically; recompute file digests, exact-set joins and duplicate ownership. Emit machine JSON before rendered Markdown. Fixtures must cover missing/extra/stale evidence, wrong subject digest, collapsed capability state, unclassified residual, false MCP certification and a private PASS incorrectly reused as public authorization.
**Validation:** Schema and validator fixtures are green; every upstream normative ID has exactly one matrix row and no unknown row; all evidence paths resolve and digests match the accepted producer receipt; mutated/missing/stale/duplicate rows turn red; private and public verdicts vary independently in fixtures; `baseline_pending` timing and `capability_unavailable` hosted attestation cannot become verified; no upstream product, receipt or decision is edited; plan lint adds no ERROR.
**Deliverables:** `release-acceptance-v1` contract/schema, exact digest-qualified acceptance matrix, deterministic validator and human finding ready for documentation and final dossier work.
