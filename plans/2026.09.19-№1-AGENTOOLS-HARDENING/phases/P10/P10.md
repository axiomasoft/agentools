# P10 — Production-grade release acceptance и owner gate

## Phase Context

> СКЕЛЕТ — не детализирован, разворачивается `design-phase: task:plan-design 2026.09.19-№1-AGENTOOLS-HARDENING P10`

P10 не добавляет функции: она связывает receipts P1–P9, проверяет документацию и clean consumers
из candidate archives, формирует final DoD и готовит обратимый private/local release candidate.
Любая внешняя публикация, tag или смена legal identity остаётся за `[OWNER-GATE:G-RELEASE]`.

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: все deliverables и evidence P1–P9.
> Производит: release dossier, consumer receipts, migration/support docs, risk acceptance и gated release decision.
> Границы: owner gate не подменяется зелёным CI.

> **Phase Assurance | v1.**
> Impact: security-external
> Integration Seams: built-archives · clean-consumers · docs-migrations · support-versioning · release-gate
> Live or Irreversible: yes
> Audit Intent: direct-close-eligible
> Rationale: independent consumers and dossier prove candidate fitness; irreversible publish remains explicit owner action and full review.

## DoR (Definition of Ready)

- [ ] P1–P9 закрыты, receipts актуальны, whole-plan design audit был GREEN до execution.
- [ ] Candidate archives проверены независимо от working tree.
- [ ] Остаточные риски имеют owner/disposition и не скрыты общим CI status.
- [ ] Legal identity, license и publication channel либо решены owner'ом, либо release остаётся private/local.

## Scope Included

README/security/durability/versioning/support/compatibility docs; migration guides; clean Composer and
Dart consumers from candidate archives; official MCP applicability evidence; final threat/guarantee
review; release notes/checklist; support matrix; rollback/revocation instructions; final DoD;
private/local RC и explicit G-RELEASE package.

## Scope Excluded

Новые runtime features; молчаливое принятие residual risk; public registry upload/tag без owner gate;
retroactive изменение AGENTOOLS; обещания, не подтверждённые receipts.

## Dependencies

P1–P9. Это терминальная acceptance-фаза PLAN1.

## Планируемый состав (черновик для детализации)

Documentation/migrations; consumer projects; evidence dossier; residual-risk adjudication;
release candidate acceptance; G-RELEASE decision packet и terminal plan reconciliation.

## Required Reads (порядок проектирования)

Все phase handoffs/receipts; `status.md`; decisions/open questions; built archives/SBOM/provenance;
README/security/support/versioning/license docs; official MCP applicability report; release policy.

## Files (ожидаемая область записи)

`research/P10-design.md`; `research/P10-design-rag.md`; release/consumer/migration/security docs,
acceptance reports, G-RELEASE packet и будущие item-спеки P10.

## Validation (ожидаемая форма)

Clean consumers используют только built archives; advertised guarantees трассируются к tests/receipts;
all accepted audit findings закрыты; no unresolved release blocker; private RC воспроизводим;
public publish невозможен без recorded owner approval G-RELEASE.
