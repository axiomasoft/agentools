### P1.4 — Заморозить guarantees, non-guarantees и proof ownership

**Intent:** Создать normative catalog конкретных security/durability/compatibility guarantees и явных non-guarantees с assumptions, evidence class и owning phase.
**Why:** Аудит прямо предупреждает, что process-local replay state, split idempotency completion и external side effects не дают `exactly-once`; без стабильного каталога P2–P10 могут тестировать разные обещания. `RAG:—` (F01–F12, current contracts); external sources constrain wording only, not current status.
**Scope Included:** Stable guarantee IDs; statement, trust assumptions, failure boundary, present status, owning phase, required negative/concurrency/crash/consumer proof; non-guarantees for external effects, availability, compromised trusted dependencies, in-memory adapters, cross-version unknown data and official MCP scope; guarantee-to-threat and guarantee-to-migration links.
**Scope Excluded:** Marking future guarantees delivered; selecting P2–P5 API/DDL; performance thresholds; marketing claims; waiving a red guarantee through prose.

**Implementation Rules:** IDs are `G-<DOMAIN>-###` and `NG-<DOMAIN>-###`; each guarantee has exactly one owning P2–P10 phase and at least one falsifying test scenario. Status enum is `baseline-red`, `baseline-partial`, `designed`, `proved`; only immutable executable evidence can yield `proved`. External-effect wording is limited to admission, replay, fencing and conditional downstream cooperation. Cross-language equality is semantic/machine-readable, not class-name symmetry.
**Code Guidance:** Write observable postconditions and refusal behavior, not aspirations such as “robust”. Separate atomic token consume from effect execution; lease ownership from exactly-once effect; atomic event append from consumer delivery; Agentools conformance from official MCP conformance; artifact provenance from runtime correctness. Link every assumption to a trust role from P1.2.
**Validation:** Unique IDs and closed statuses; every open `TM-###` is addressed by a guarantee or explicit non-guarantee; every guarantee names one owner, assumptions, failure boundary and proof carrier; every P2–P10 phase owns at least one relevant row or is explicitly `acceptance-only`; forbidden unqualified claims (`exactly-once`, `secure`, `production-ready`, `MCP compliant`) count zero.
**Deliverables:** Normative guarantee/non-guarantee catalog that downstream item specs can cite by stable ID and P10 can reconcile against actual receipts.
